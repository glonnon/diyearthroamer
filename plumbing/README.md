# Plumbing

P&ID (`pid.drawio`) and fixture/tank schedules live here. PEX-A
distribution with manifold + per-fixture shutoffs.

## Tank schedule (locked)

| Tank | Capacity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh | 100–120 gal | May be 60+60 split for chassis fit and side balance; heated, insulated, low + forward |
| Grey | 30–40 gal | Heated, baffled, 3" outlet to dump panel |
| Black | 30–40 gal | Heated, full-size cleanout, tank rinse, true roof vent |

## Manifold

- PEX-A from Uponor or Apollo, expansion fittings, no crimp.
- Color-coded red/blue.
- Manifold with shutoff per fixture.
- Drain points at all low spots; blow-out fitting at manifold.

## Heat sources

- DHW: hydronic loop from diesel heater (ADR-0007).
- Tank heating: pads on fresh / grey / black; thermostatic.
- Wet-bay loop: hydronic bypass keeps service bay above freezing.

## Compliance / best practice

- Vacuum breaker on toilet supply.
- Backflow prevention on city water inlet (regulator + check).
- True roof vent for black; AAV only where unavoidable on grey.
