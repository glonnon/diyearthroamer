# ADR-0005: Sleeping — Fixed Cabover Bed

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-07
- **Related:** REQUIREMENTS.md §13; ADR-0001, ADR-0002

## Context

Two adults, full-time-capable, want a permanent bed that doesn't
require nightly conversion and frees the dinette/galley for daytime
use. Cabover layout puts the bed over the F550 cab, freeing the
21' shell floor for living, galley, bath, and the systems bay.

## Options considered

1. **Fixed cabover bed (chosen).** Permanent, frees floor area, classic
   Earthroamer profile.
2. **East-west fixed bed at the rear.** Conflicts with the bike lift
   box and consumes rear floor.
3. **Convertible dinette/bed.** Adds nightly friction; rejected.

## Decision

Fixed cabover bed over the crew cab roof. Target king (76"x80") if the
cabover footprint allows; otherwise queen (60"x80").

## Consequences

- Cab pass-through (gasketed, insulated) opens at the cabover front
  bulkhead onto the rear bench area of the crew cab.
- Mattress base with vented slats or coir mat for condensation
  control on the cold cabover floor.
- Headboard side gets USB-C, switches, reading lights.
