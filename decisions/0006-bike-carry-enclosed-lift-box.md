# ADR-0006: Bike Carry — Enclosed Motorized Lift-Box

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-07
- **Related:** REQUIREMENTS.md §20

## Context

Carry 2–3 high-end MTB / gravel bikes, secure from theft, protected
from weather and dust, on rough roads. No interest in giving up
interior living space to a bike garage.

## Options considered

1. **Internal rear garage in shell.** Loses living space; tracks dust
   into cabin; rejected given the 21' shell layout.
2. **Flatbed garage box (30" deep) aft of shell.** Rejected this round —
   adds rear-axle weight at long lever arm and complicates departure
   angle.
3. **External motorized rack (Aluminess-style).** Bikes exposed to
   weather; theft risk; ok as a fallback only.
4. **Enclosed motorized lift-box (chosen).** Weather-tight, lockable,
   low-loading height when deployed, raised to clearance position when
   stowed.

## Decision

Custom enclosed lift-box at the rear, mounted on dual linear actuators
(or hydraulic micro-pack — O5), capacity 2–3 bikes.

## Consequences

- Significant fabrication: hitch-class structural carrier, lift
  kinematics, sealed enclosure, interlocks, manual override.
- Adds rear weight; mitigated by forward weight bias (§1) and by
  selecting aluminum for the box.
- Requires separate ADR for actuator vs hydraulic drive (O5).

## Open questions

- O5: actuator vs hydraulic drive.
- E-bike charger powering plan when boondocking.
- Spare-tire interaction (carrier or under-bed mount?).
