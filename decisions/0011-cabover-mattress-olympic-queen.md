# ADR-0011: Cabover Mattress — Olympic Queen (66" × 80")

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-07
- **Related:** ADR-0005; `cad/20-shell/cabover-fit.md`

## Context

Closes O2. Width and depth study against the 21' shell interior
(88" wide) and cabover platform depth (~77") shows queen fits easily,
king fits with only 1" depth margin and loses headboard storage,
Olympic queen sits comfortably in between.

## Decision

Olympic queen (66" × 80") laid transverse across the cabover platform.

## Consequences

- ~11" per side of cabinet/cubby space.
- ~11" of depth margin for headboard with USB/PD, switches, reading
  lights, small shelf.
- Custom mattress sourcing required (less common than queen/king);
  latex or hybrid from a maker that does custom sizes — already
  planned per REQUIREMENTS.md §13.
- Pass-through clear opening (24" × 24") fits in this layout.

## Open questions

- Final mattress vendor + thickness (target 8–10" with vented base).
