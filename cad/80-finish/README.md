# 80-finish — Flooring, Wall Panels, Ceiling, Trim

Cosmetic + finish layer over structural cabinetry and shell.

- Flooring: luxury vinyl plank rated for radiant heat (over
  PEX-Al-PEX assembly, see `cad/60-hvac/`)
- Wall panels: FRP at impact zones; upholstered or veneer elsewhere
- Ceiling: acoustic fabric or FRP, integrated lighting tracks
- Window trim, door trim, insulated curtains/shades
